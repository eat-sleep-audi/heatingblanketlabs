HeatingBlanketLabs develops privacy-focused software and mobile applications. We believe your personal information should remain yours.

As a general principle, our applications are designed to minimize or eliminate the collection of personal information whenever possible.

Information We Collect

Most HeatingBlanketLabs applications do not collect personal information, account information, call logs, contact data, messages, location history, or other personally identifiable information.

If an application requires access to a device permission, that permission is used only for the functionality described within the application.

How Information Is Used

Any information processed by our applications is used solely to provide the application's intended functionality.

We do not sell personal information, rent user information, or share user data with advertising networks.

Data Storage

Where possible, application activity remains on the user's device. Many HeatingBlanketLabs applications operate entirely without cloud services or remote servers.

Any app-specific data handling practices will be disclosed within that application's individual privacy policy.

Third-Party Services

Some applications may rely on third-party platform services such as Google Play, Android system services, or payment processing systems.

Those services operate under their own privacy policies and terms.

Children's Privacy

HeatingBlanketLabs applications are not directed toward children under the age of 13 unless explicitly stated otherwise.

Security

We strive to build software using privacy-first principles and reasonable security practices designed to protect user information.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date.

Contact

Questions regarding this Privacy Policy may be directed to HeatingBlanketLabs through the contact information provided on our website or app store listings.
